FPO/FPC Registration Guide

Group Selling ek season ke liye hota hai — agar aap permanent Farmer Producer Company (FPC) banana chahte hain, to yahan poora process hai.

🏢 What is an FPO/FPC?

A Farmer Producer Organisation (FPO), usually registered as a Farmer Producer Company (FPC), is a legal entity owned by farmer-members themselves — for buying inputs in bulk, negotiating better prices, and accessing credit and government schemes collectively.

✅ Eligibility
  • Minimum 10 individual farmers (or 2 producer institutions) needed to register
  • Registered under Chapter XXIA of the Companies Act, 2013
  • Members should generally be small/marginal farmers from the same area or crop cluster
Step-by-Step Process
1. Form a Farmer Group

Gather at least 10 farmers willing to join, ideally growing similar crops in the same area.

2. Choose a Name & Draft Documents

Pick a company name and prepare the Memorandum & Articles of Association describing your FPC's objectives.

3. Register on the MCA Portal

File via SPICe+ on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) V3 portal, along with member KYC documents.

4. Get Support from a CBBO

Cluster-Based Business Organisations (CBBOs), appointed by NABARD, SFAC, or NCDC, can guide and even help fund the registration process.

5. Apply for Government Support

Once registered, apply through SFAC/NABARD for equity grants, credit guarantees, and management support under the govt's 10,000 FPO scheme.

💰 Financial Support Available
Equity Grant (matching)Up to ₹15 lakh
Management Support (5 years)Up to ₹18 lakh
Credit GuaranteeUp to ₹2 crore
Registration cost (one-time)~₹15,000–₹40,000

Agricultural income is tax-exempt for FPCs; other income is taxed at a concessional rate. Figures are general guidance — confirm current details with SFAC/NABARD before proceeding.
