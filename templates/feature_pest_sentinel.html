Pest & Disease Sentinel Network

📡🐛
Proposed / R&D Concept
🎯 What it does

A field-deployed network of low-cost cameras and sensors, paired with on-device (edge) AI, that continuously watches crops for early signs of pest and disease outbreaks. Instead of a farmer scouting one field manually, the network scans many plots around the clock and flags trouble the moment it starts — well before it spreads.

Try the demo
📷

Only crop/leaf/field photos are accepted — other photos will be marked invalid.

How it works
📷 1. Sense

Solar-powered camera/sensor nodes placed across fields capture leaf, canopy, and pheromone-trap images on a regular schedule.

🧠 2. Detect on the edge

A lightweight AI model running directly on the device (no constant internet needed) classifies pest and disease signs locally, so alerts still work in low-connectivity villages.

🗺️ 3. Map hotspots

Detections from multiple nodes are combined to identify outbreak hotspots and predict the likely direction of spread across neighbouring farms.

💊 4. Guide the response

Instead of blanket spraying, the system recommends precise, localized bio-intervention — the right treatment, right dose, right spot — cutting pesticide overuse.

🔔 5. Alert the farmer

Simple local-language alerts (voice/SMS/app) reach the farmer with what was found and what to do next — designed for the same AASHA multilingual assistant already in AgroStar.

Why it matters

Early detection can save entire harvests, reduces unnecessary pesticide spraying (better for soil, water, and farmer health), and turns pest management from reactive fire-fighting into proactive, data-driven prevention — at a cost small and marginal farmers can actually afford.

Fits into AgroStar

This complements the existing Pest & Disease Detection feature — that tool diagnoses a single uploaded photo on demand, while the Sentinel Network automates and scales that detection continuously across a whole field or village.
