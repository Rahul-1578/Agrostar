e-NAM Integration Info

🌐 What is e-NAM?

e-NAM (National Agriculture Market) is the government's online trading portal that links mandis across India into one unified digital market. Instead of only buyers physically present at your local mandi bidding on your produce, buyers from other integrated mandis can bid too — often meaning a better price through more competition.

Launched in 2016 • Portal: enam.gov.in

How to Register
1. Visit enam.gov.in

Click "Farmer Registration" and start the sign-up process.

2. Provide Your Details

Aadhaar number, mobile number, state, and your nearest APMC/mandi.

3. Add Bank Account

Add your bank account (IFSC + account number) — sale proceeds are paid directly here, no middleman involved.

4. Verify with OTP

Confirm via OTP — your account becomes active immediately. Registration is completely free.

5. Bring Produce for Assaying

At the mandi, your produce is quality-assayed before it goes up for online bidding, so buyers see a verified grade.
