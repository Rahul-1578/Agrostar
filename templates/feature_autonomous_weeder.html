Autonomous Precision Weeder

🤖🌱
Proposed / R&D Concept
🎯 What it does

A small, low-cost autonomous machine that drives itself along crop rows, tells crop seedlings apart from weeds, and removes weeds mechanically — without herbicide — built to be shared across several small farms in a village rather than owned by one.

How it works
🧭 1. Row navigation

The machine uses onboard cameras/sensors to detect crop rows and drives itself down each one, staying aligned even on uneven field terrain.

👁️ 2. Crop vs. weed ID

An onboard vision model tells the difference between the crop plant and surrounding weeds in real time as it moves.

✂️ 3. Precision weeding

A small mechanical tool targets and removes only the identified weeds — close to the crop row, without harming the crop and without spraying chemicals.

🤝 4. Shared, cooperative use

Sized and priced for a farmer cooperative or custom-hiring center to own jointly and schedule/booked turn-by-turn across member farms — bringing automation within reach of small landholdings that could never justify buying one alone.

Why it matters

Weeding is one of the most labour-intensive and costly parts of crop care for small farmers. A shared autonomous weeder cuts labour cost and herbicide use, and a cooperative ownership model makes precision-agriculture hardware affordable at the scale of India's small and marginal farms.

Fits into AgroStar

Booking and cost-sharing for the machine could plug into the existing Farmer Community feature, letting nearby farmers coordinate a shared weeding schedule directly in the app.
