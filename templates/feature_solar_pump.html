PM-KUSUM Solar Pump Scheme

☀️ About the Scheme

PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan) helps farmers move from diesel to solar irrigation pumps. The Central Government gives a fixed 30% subsidy, the State Government adds at least 30% more, and the farmer covers the remaining share — often just 10% upfront with a bank loan covering the rest.

Exact state subsidy percentage varies — check your state's renewable energy portal for the current rate before applying.
